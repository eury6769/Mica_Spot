About

Mica Spot is a website built to assist students and school personnels alike on discovering delicious local food spots, specifically here in Saint Micheals Collage of Laguna. A simple act of browsing for potential spots for lunch or break is much more important than some may think. The amount of time people consume endlessly scrolling for the perfect place to eat is a hassle that often leads to rush and unenjoyable meal due to the time wasted contimplating where and what to eat.

Mica Spot is another outlet for our local business owners to promote and advertise their goods. The website allows people to rate and give their comments to their favorite Karinderyas and Cafes. The purpose of this website is not only to help people look up tasty food spots to visit and try, but to bring the community together by bringing these business to potential customers and vice-versa. We hope that Mica Spot becomes a way for people to connect, enjoy, and discover heartful meals in our local community here in Binan.
